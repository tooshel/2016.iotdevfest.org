IoT Devfest was a single track event focused on the Internet of Things (IoT). We had speakers flying in from all over the country (okay, mostly California) and of course some of our very own local talent. We had many amazing sponsors including Google, Octoblu and LocalMotors, Intel Software and Microchip. The event was on Jan 9th at the Mesa Arts Center and registration is now closed.
